1. Introduction & Overview

Welcome to WordsCapes - Word Connect Puzzle (package name com.blueapps.wordscapescon), created and maintained by BlueAppsBox ("we", "our", or "us").

This Privacy Policy explains how information is processed and safeguarded when you install and play WordsCapes - Word Connect Puzzle on mobile Android devices. We adhere to the Google Play Developer Distribution Agreement, Google Play Families Policy, and international privacy standards (GDPR, COPPA, and CCPA).

Core Commitment: WordsCapes does not require user accounts, email registration, passwords, or personal identity verification. We never sell, rent, or trade your personal data.

2. Online Nature & Connectivity

WordsCapes - Word Connect Puzzle is an online casual puzzle game. While crossword grid mechanics are solved instantaneously on your device, the game requires active internet connectivity (via Wi-Fi or cellular mobile data) to:

  • Communicate with Google Mobile Ads (AdMob) SDK to request and display non-intrusive banner ads, app open ads, and rewarded video ads.
  • Credit rewarded video ad tokens (free letters, shuffles, and coins) to the player upon verified ad completion.
  • Perform anonymous stability checks and ensure up-to-date gameplay validation.

3. Data We Do NOT Collect (Zero Sensitive PII)

We practice strict data minimization. The game does NOT request or collect any of the following sensitive personal information:

  • No Real Identity Data: No first name, last name, home address, physical location, or national identification numbers.
  • No Account Credentials: No usernames, email addresses, or passwords required to play.
  • No Device Sensors: No access to camera, microphone, audio recordings, or biometric sensors.
  • No Social Media or Contacts: No access to phone contacts, address books, call logs, or social media profiles.
  • No Financial / Payment Data: The game does not process credit cards, bank accounts, or real-money in-app transactions.

4. On-Device Local Data Storage

To preserve your gameplay achievements across all 300 levels, WordsCapes uses client-side HTML5 localStorage and Android app-specific internal sandbox storage. The following data is saved purely on your physical device:

Stored Property Purpose Location
Level Progress (1 to 300) Resumes your active chapter and unlocked levels. On-device localStorage
Virtual Coins & Star Ratings Tracks virtual hints, unlocked bonus chest rewards, and stage mastery. On-device localStorage
Daily Free Hint Timestamps Enables 1 free hint and 1 free shuffle every calendar day. On-device localStorage
Sound & Audio Preferences Remembers sound on/off toggle settings. On-device localStorage
Virtual Coins Notice: Virtual coins have zero monetary value, cannot be exchanged for fiat currency or cash rewards, and are purely visual aids for crossword letter hints.

5. Third-Party Advertising (Google AdMob)

WordsCapes - Word Connect Puzzle integrates the official Google Mobile Ads SDK (AdMob) to display advertisements that support ongoing development. These ad formats include:

  • Banner Ads: Displayed neatly at the bottom of the game container.
  • Cold-Start App Open Ads: Displayed briefly when the game launches.
  • Rewarded Video Ads (User-Initiated): Players may choose to view short 5-second video ads in exchange for free in-game letter hints, shuffles, or extra attempts after 3 wrong turns.

Google AdMob may process non-sensitive technical identifiers including:

  • Google Advertising ID (AAID), subject to your Android device OS privacy toggle.
  • IP address (for coarse geographic location / country-level ad serving).
  • Device model, OS version, and network carrier metrics.

To review Google's advertising policies or opt out of personalized advertising on Android, visit:
https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads

7. User Control & Data Deletion

Because all game progress is stored locally on your device, you have complete and instantaneous control over your data:

  • Clear All Data: Open your Android device Settings > Apps > WordsCapes > Storage > Clear Storage / Clear Cache. This immediately deletes all saved levels and coins.
  • Uninstall: Uninstalling WordsCapes from your device automatically removes all local files associated with the game.
  • Ad ID Reset: You can reset or delete your Google Advertising ID at any time via Android Settings > Privacy > Ads > Delete Advertising ID.
